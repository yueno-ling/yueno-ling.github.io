## About Me

I am a student of linguistics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by [Professor Juan Uriagereka](https://linguistics.umd.edu/directory/juan-uriagereka) and [Professor Naomi Feldman](https://linguistics.umd.edu/directory/naomi-feldman). I investigate the formal properties of the human faculty of language through the logical reconstruction and comparative study of individual I-languages, including Japanese, French, English, and Classical Chinese, among others. My work ranges from traditional descriptive studies to theoretical applications of abstract mathematics.

## Research Interests

- **Linguistics**: generative theory of human language, syntax in the broad/narrow sense
- **Metatheory of linguistics**: empirical grounding of linguistic theory, logical structure of linguistic theory
- **Mathematics**: general topology, commutative algebra, algebraic geometry, category theory
